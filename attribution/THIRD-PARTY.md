# Third-party attribution and provenance

**Group 6 · VeriFin: Evidence-Grounded Financial Research with Structured Verification**

## Application and research methods

- The submitted VeriFin release `503c149808aeaf225f4b35be89c89dd50a827fbc` uses Python 3.11 or later, its standard-library SQLite and HTTP modules, and a local browser interface. Direct optional dependencies are declared in `code/pyproject.toml`. Disclosure parsing uses lxml and pdfplumber. Structured HTML and embedding studies additionally use lxml, pandas, Sentence Transformers, Hugging Face Transformers, and PyTorch. Historical extraction metadata also identifies pypdf for preserved PDF text. See `code/docs/OPEN_SOURCE_COMPONENTS_2026-11-06.md` and `code/requirements-embeddings-macos-arm64.lock.txt` for the recorded experiment environment.
- The separate orchestration experiment uses LangChain's MIT-licensed LangGraph and its SQLite checkpointer for graph scheduling and persistence. The selected submission release uses the project's Python controller. Deep Agents was reviewed as a candidate. BM25S was tested in a separate prototype. Refer to `evaluation/framework-selection.md` and `code/docs/OPEN_SOURCE_COMPONENTS_2026-11-06.md` for the boundary of those evaluations. [LangGraph license](https://github.com/langchain-ai/langgraph/blob/main/LICENSE) · [Deep Agents license](https://github.com/langchain-ai/deepagents/blob/main/LICENSE) · [BM25S license](https://github.com/xhluca/bm25s/blob/main/LICENSE)
- The proposal credits Shunyu Yao et al.'s ReAct for acting on tool observations, Luyu Gao et al.'s RARR for evidence-based revision, and Tianyu Gao, Howard Yen, Jiatong Yu, and Danqi Chen's ALCE for evaluating citation quality separately from answer correctness. These references informed the design. [ReAct](https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.03629) · [RARR](https://aclanthology.org/2023.acl-long.910/) · [ALCE](https://aclanthology.org/2023.emnlp-main.398/)
- Local component studies used Qwen3-Embedding-0.6B, multilingual-e5-small, BGE-M3 dense embeddings, Qwen3-Reranker-0.6B, and Perplexity's `pplx-embed-v2-late-0.6b`. Pinned source and model revisions, licenses, and the scope of actual tests appear in `code/docs/OPEN_SOURCE_MODELS_2026-11-06.sources.json` and companion research notes. The hosted generative models and their use are disclosed in [AI-use declaration](AI-USE.md).

## Financial sources

- U.S. SEC EDGAR submissions, XBRL Company Facts, filings, and company-authored earnings release exhibits provide structured numbers and documentary evidence. The source snapshot also uses Microsoft and NVIDIA investor-relations releases and performance or CFO commentary. Canonical source URLs, retrieval times, publication precision, and hashes are listed where available in `code/data/replay/manifest.json` and the evaluation manifests. The exact replay coverage is recorded in those manifests. [SEC EDGAR data APIs](https://www.sec.gov/edgar/sec-api-documentation)
- Legacy frozen market-price evidence uses Yahoo Finance chart `quote.close`. The project records the original provider fields and source URLs and flags unverified adjustment and redistribution terms. The optional Alpha Vantage adapter uses the provider's daily-data API and requires separately configured access. The historical adapter tests included synthetic fixtures and mocked responses. Documentation and source usage are recorded in `code/docs/MARKET.md`. [Alpha Vantage documentation](https://www.alphavantage.co/documentation/)
- The Apple March 2025 income-statement parser fixture comes from the EdgarTools project's pinned test-fixture mirror at `107d182146ccf5d22a4e71f82cde8b363b212cf1`. The fixture is Apple issuer material. The EdgarTools software license and the canonical SEC source are recorded separately in `code/docs/OPEN_SOURCE_EXTRA_NOTICES.txt` and `code/data/evaluation/parser_fixture_manifest.json`. [EdgarTools fixture](https://github.com/dgunning/edgartools/tree/107d182146ccf5d22a4e71f82cde8b363b212cf1/tests/fixtures/attachments/aapl/20250329) · [Apple SEC filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/320193/000032019325000057/aapl-20250329.htm)
